When Na-Gyunn watches his twin brother, Na-Jin, get brutally murdered right before his eyes, his entire world shatters. Driven by an unyielding desire for retribution, Na-Gyunn makes a dangerous choice: he assumes his dead brother's identity to infiltrate the prestigious knight-in-training academy where Na-Jin once thrived. However, swapping lives is far easier than mastering swordplay. While his brother possessed immense natural talent and formidable combat skills, Na-Gyunn lacks any intrinsic fighting prowess, forcing him to rely entirely on fierce determination, psychological warfare, and sheer grit just to stay alive.

Operating in high-stakes environments where a single slip-up means instant exposure and death, Na-Gyunn must navigate deadly trials, fierce rivals, and suspicious peers who are slowly catching onto his act. The narrative expertly blends high-octane action with strategic mind games, as the protagonist must outsmart enemies who are physically superior in every single way. Every chapter builds suffocating tension, forcing readers to question how long an ordinary, talentless boy can successfully masquerade as a legendary prodigy without cracking under the pressure.

Alternative Names: The Ember Knight

Key Characters and Dynamics

Na-Gyunn: The story's cunning protagonist. Lacking physical talent, he makes up for his weaknesses through acute observation, psychological manipulation, and relentless self-discipline to pull off the ultimate identity theft.
Na-Jin: Na-Gyunn's murdered twin brother. Regarded as a prodigious knight with exceptional skill, his legacy forms the heavy armor that Na-Gyunn must desperately try to wear without getting crushed.
